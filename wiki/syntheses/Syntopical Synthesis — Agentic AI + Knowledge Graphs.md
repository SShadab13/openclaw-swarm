### 2. Inspectional Reading
Key passages extracted from each book using chunk importance scoring. Focus on definition-heavy, key-concept, and actionable passages.

### 3. Bring Authors to Terms
Cross-book concept mapping performed. Found concepts spanning multiple books — these form the "neutral language" for swarm discussion.

### 4. Define the Issues
5 framed questions that all authors answer (explicitly or implicitly):

1. **What defines an AI agent vs a traditional AI system?**
2. **What role do knowledge graphs play in agentic systems?**
3. **How do agents maintain state, memory, and continuity?**
4. **What is the relationship between LLMs and agent reasoning?**
5. **How should multi-agent systems coordinate (swarm behavior)?**

### 5. Analyze the Discussion
- **Agreements:** Core concepts universally accepted across 4+ books
- **Disagreements:** Unique perspectives held by single books
- **Open Questions:** Controversies not yet resolved by the literature

## Unified Understanding (The Syntopicon)

> *This understanding exists in NONE of the individual books. It is constructed by the reader through syntopical reading.*

### Core Definition
An AI agent is an autonomous system that **perceives, reasons, and acts** to achieve goals, often using LLMs for cognition and knowledge graphs for structured memory.

### Key Components
1. **LLM-based reasoning** — The cognitive engine
2. **Tool use and action execution** — The ability to affect the world
3. **Knowledge graphs for structured memory** — Long-term, queryable state
4. **Multi-agent coordination (swarms)** — Distributed intelligence
5. **State management and continuity** — Temporal coherence across sessions

### Live Controversies
1. **Autonomy vs Oversight** — How much should agents decide vs humans?
2. **Symbolic vs Neural** — Balance between KG (symbolic) and LLM (neural) reasoning
3. **Centralized vs Decentralized** — Should swarms have a queen/coordinator or be peer-to-peer?

### Open Questions
1. How to ensure agent alignment with human values?
2. What is the optimal KG schema for agent memory?
3. How to measure agent "intelligence" vs mere task completion?
